QuantaraX

QuantaraX was an experimental non-profit organization aimed at developing and testing peer-learning techniques and rediscovery methods of learning in the field of Quantum Mechanics.

QuantaraX has been sunset in its current form. The experiment has now ended after gathering its intended insights. But the mission continues through other channels.

Our Achievements

What QuantaraX accomplished during its experimental run as a community-driven quantum education initiative.

01

15+ QM & Rediscovery Sessions

Organized fifteen plus quantum mechanics and rediscovery sessions, both online and in-person, bringing learners together to explore quantum concepts collaboratively.

02

ReDiscovery Method (Grade 11)

Developed and tested ReDiscovery Method techniques on Grade 11th students in Pakistan. Reports prepared for release to support others in adopting the method.

03

QM Discord Community

Created the second largest Discord community dedicated to Quantum Mechanics, with over 100 members. The community continues to exist today.

04

Particle Physics Game Prototype

Created the first ever particle physics game based on the rediscovery method as a working prototype.

05

Data & Experience Sharing

Provided data and experience sharing to various Pakistani organizations for their upcoming projects related to the Rediscovery method.

The first-in-the-field rediscovery-based mathematics olympiad at Dr. Salam Society of Mathematics, Government College University Lahore. QuantaraX's Rediscovery methods guided the whole paper, and a theorem was discovered at the end.
